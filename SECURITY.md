# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability, please do not report it via public GitHub issues. Instead, please report it via the following process:

1. **Email:** Send a detailed report to [security@example.com](mailto:security@example.com) (Replace with actual contact).
2. **Details to include:** 
    - A description of the vulnerability.
    - Steps to reproduce (including a minimal PoC if possible).
    - The potential impact of the vulnerability.
    - The version/commit of AgentFence affected.

## Our Commitment

- **Acknowledgement:** We will acknowledge receipt of your report within [X] business days.
- **Triage:** We will investigate the report and keep you updated on our progress.
- **Remediation:** We aim to resolve critical vulnerabilities as quickly as possible.
- **Disclosure:** We follow a coordinated disclosure process. We will work with you to fix the issue before making any information public.

## Scope

We welcome research into the following areas:
- Authorization bypasses
- Policy confusion or bypasses
- Identity/descriptor spoofing
- Audit/receipt forgery
- Unsafe fail-open behavior

**Out of Scope:**
- Social engineering attacks
- Attacks on third-party services
- Denial of Service (DoS) attacks that do not impact the security boundary
- Generic quality bugs or UI/UX issues
